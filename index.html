Affected by an incident?
Here is where to turn.

Urgent

Need help? Call a hotline

If you are facing active violence or intimidation, the national election emergency line is staffed around the clock. Ask for the desk nearest your polling unit.

Illustration
Legal

Get legal aid

Accredited election lawyers review disputed and contested incidents free of charge, and can carry a case through to the election tribunal on your behalf.

Illustration
Wellbeing

Get trauma and safety counseling

Confidential counseling for voters, observers and officials affected by election-day violence. You do not need to have filed a report to talk to someone.

Donate to a legal fund

Fund tribunal representation for the voters and observers who came forward with evidence and cannot afford a lawyer to see the case through.
